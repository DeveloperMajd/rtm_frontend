Privacy Policy — RTM

Last updated: September 2026

RTM is a real-time messaging application built as a personal portfolio project. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used. It is not a commercial product — there is no advertising, no analytics tracking, and no data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

What data is collected

  • Account information: name, email address, and a securely hashed password (or, for Google/Facebook sign-in, the name, email, and profile picture your provider makes available).
  • Profile information: an optional bio and avatar image you choose to upload.
  • Messages and attachments: the content of messages you send, and any images/files you attach to them, stored so they can be delivered to the people you're messaging.
  • Contacts: the list of other users you've added as contacts.
  • Presence data: whether you're currently online and typing, which is ephemeral and not permanently stored.

How it's used

Solely to operate the application: authenticating you, delivering messages to their intended recipients, showing your name/avatar to people you message, and sending you a password-reset email if you request one. Nothing here is used for advertising or analytics.

Third-party services

The app is hosted using a small number of infrastructure providers, each of which processes data only to the extent needed to run the service:

  • Neon — hosts the application's database
  • Upstash — hosts ephemeral session/cache data
  • Resend — delivers transactional email (e.g. password reset links)
  • Google / Facebook — if you choose to sign in with one of these, they authenticate your identity; RTM only receives your name, email, and profile picture, never your password
  • Vercel / Oracle Cloud — host the application itself

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. Since this is a portfolio project rather than a production service with a self-service account-deletion flow yet, you can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing the address below.
